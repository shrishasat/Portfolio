ABOUT ME

I work where
brains meet machines.

interested in understanding how information is represented in the brain and how we can build machines that learn from it.

01

Background

My work sits between neuroscience, machine learning and brain-computer interfaces, with a particular interest in extracting meaningful representations from non-invasive brain signals.

02

Recent work

Recently I've been working with MEG to investigate how language, meaning and iconicity are represented in the brain. I've worked with neural encoding, representational similarity analysis, temporal response functions and computational language representations.

Alongside research, I've been building small computational tools, experimenting with GPU-accelerated analysis and exploring the intersection between neuroscience and AI.

03

What interests me

Non-invasive neural decoding. Representation learning. Brain-computer interfaces. Computational models of cognition. And the bigger question of how much of the mind can be inferred from the signals it leaves behind.
