ABOUT

I work at the edge of brains and machines.

I'm Shrisha Sathishskumar — I work in neuroAI, the stretch of ground where neuroscience, machine learning, and a healthy amount of curiosity overlap. Most days that means reading brain-computer-interface papers, building small models, and asking too many "but why does it work" questions.

What I'm drawn to

Non-invasive brain decoding, representation learning, and the question of how much of cognition is legible from the outside. I like problems that sit between disciplines, where the interesting part is translating one field's language into another's.

How I work

Slowly, then all at once. I keep a running journal of papers and half-formed ideas — some of it ends up in the blog. I'd rather understand one idea deeply than skim ten.

Outside of work

Reading, long scrolls through arXiv, and the reading corner on my blog page, where I keep a running list of books worth your time.
